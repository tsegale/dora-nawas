Fauna of Doro !Nawas

Desert-Adapted Specialists

Beyond the black rhino, Doro !Nawas Conservancy shelters an extraordinary diversity of species uniquely specialized for survival in semi-arid and desert environments.

Aba-Huab River

Desert-Adapted Elephants

Renowned for their elongated limbs and wider footpads, these gentle giants dig shallow wells in dry riverbeds to access subterranean water, sustaining entire ecosystems.

Plateaus & Slopes

Hartmann's Mountain Zebra

Agile rock climbers equipped with fast-growing, pointed hooves. They traverse sheer basalt cliffs and gather in herds along gravel plains at sundown.

Critically Endangered

Desert Black Rhino

The iconic emblem of our conservancy. Browsing euphorbia bushes and walking hundreds of kilometres between ancient spring waters.

Authentic Guided by local community trackers who grew up in these valleys.
Safari Activities

Unforgettable Damaraland Journeys

Whether traversing rocky valleys in specialized open 4x4 safari vehicles or walking softly behind an expert tracker on the spoor of black rhinos, every day in Doro !Nawas offers a deeply immersive African wilderness experience.

✓ Morning Guided Rhino Tracking: Early dawn tracking expeditions with local rangers and SRT field guides.
✓ Aba-Huab River Game Drives: Following dry sand rivers lined with ancient ana trees, encountering breeding herds of desert elephants.
✓ Twyfelfontein Excursions: Guided walks through the ancient rock engraving amphitheatre.
✓ Sundowner Drives: Toasting the setting sun atop red sandstone kopjes with panoramic vistas of the Brandberg.

Celestial Splendour

Sleep Out Under the Milky Way

Far removed from urban light pollution, the night sky over Doro !Nawas Conservancy is a breathtaking celestial theatre. Guests staying at eco-lodges such as Wilderness Doro Nawas can roll their beds out onto private rooftop terraces.

Drift off under a shimmering canopy of millions of stars, with crystal-clear views of the Southern Cross, the Magellanic Clouds, and the radiant arch of the Milky Way, punctuated only by the distant call of a gecko or jackal.

Practical Advice

Planning Your Safari Experience

Best Season

May to October (Dry Season): Mild daytime temperatures (24–28°C), cool starry nights, and optimal wildlife concentrations around permanent desert springs.

Vehicle Requirements

A high-clearance 4x4 vehicle is strongly recommended for secondary gravel roads and river tracks (D2612 and C39). Carry two spare tires and ample drinking water.

Packing Essentials

Neutral khaki/earth-toned walking clothing (no bright whites or neon colors for rhino tracking), sturdy ankle-support boots, wide-brim sunhat, UV sunscreen, and quality 8x42 binoculars.
