Getting Here

Travel Routes & Accessibility

Doro !Nawas is accessible via well-maintained scenic gravel routes and by private air charter directly into our licensed airstrip.

Self-Drive Route 1

From Windhoek (450 km)

Take the B1 north to Otjiwarongo, then the C38 to Outjo. Turn onto the C39 westward through Khorixas. Approximately 80 km past Khorixas, turn onto the D2612 heading toward Twyfelfontein. Estimated driving time: 5.5 to 6 hours.

Self-Drive Route 2

From Swakopmund (330 km)

Drive north along the Atlantic coast on the C34 to Henties Bay, then take the C35 inland past Uis and the Brandberg massif. Continue on the C35 and turn left onto the D2612 toward Twyfelfontein. Estimated driving time: 4 to 4.5 hours.

Fly-In Safari

Doro Nawas Airstrip

Registered, compacted gravel airstrip suitable for light aircraft (e.g. Cessna 206, 208 Caravan). Coordinates: 20°28'25"S, 14°23'30"E. Runway length: 1,600 metres. Wilderness lodge transfers meet arriving flights.
